Community Bookmarks lists {{< count >}} tools, Apple documentation pages, blogs, podcasts, conferences and communities for people who manage Apple devices. You can search it, filter it by category, import it into your browser as bookmarks, or reuse the data as JSON.

## Where it comes from

Community Bookmarks grew out of [The Apple Community Bookmarks](https://github.com/cbookmarks/bookmarks), a bookmarks file curated by the Apple community and forked from Navigating Apple. It now also includes the links from [The Definitive MacAdmins resource directory](https://github.com/maccy10/definitivemacadmins).

Thank you to everyone who contributed to either list.

## How it's maintained

Every resource is an entry in a YAML file in the {{< repo "" "GitHub repository" >}}. Anyone can suggest a change through a form; maintainers review it, and GitHub Actions take care of the rest: adding the entry, checking it, rebuilding the site and checking every link once a week.

The site is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io) and hosted on GitHub Pages. There is no tracking and no advertising.
